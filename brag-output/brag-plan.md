## 1. Project Planning Rubric

### Q1: What is it?
The **SupeRR Selector Season 2 Rajasthan Royals IPL 2027 Pressure Analytics War Room** is an end-to-end algorithmic intelligence system and live interactive dashboard designed to evaluate, rank, and target high-clutch T20 cricket players under extreme match leverage.

### Q2: Who is it for, and what does it do for them?
- **Audience**: Rajasthan Royals executive leadership, head coach, chief scout, and auction table bidders.
- **Value Delivered**: Eliminates the "clutch penalty" by identifying stars whose performance elevates during 20th-over nail-biters while warning against overpaying for players who collapse under fire.

### Q3: What sets it apart?
- **Multi-Factor Pressure Quantification**: Rather than relying on static season averages, every delivery is evaluated with situational leverage via the **Match Pressure Index (MPI)** and **Context-Adjusted Value Added (CAVA)**.
- **Machine Learning Matchup Simulation**: Predicts discrete ball-by-ball outcome probabilities (0, 1, 2, 4, 6, W) for any batsman vs bowler combination in real time.
- **Strict Compliance**: 100% exclusion of Pakistan national players (67 identified and removed), Sanju Samson mapped to CSK per contract row 5462, and senior Indian internationals accurately classified as Capped Indians.

### Q4: The Hook
*"Raw averages lie. A fifty scored when the match is already lost means nothing compared to 20 runs scored under extreme match pressure. Pressure reveals the truth."*

### Q5: Visual Identity
- **Palette**: Rajasthan Royals Dark Neon theme (`#070B14` Dark Navy background, `#EA1A85` Royals Pink, `#254AA5` Royals Royal Blue, `#38BDF8` Neon Cyan, `#10B981` Emerald Green).
- **Typography**: Clean Segoe UI / Inter sans-serif with high contrast and zero emojis.
